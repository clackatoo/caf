Theme

How it's built

  • One rounded box. The message is on top, the controls are along the bottom. Nothing sits above the text.
  • Bottom left: the agent's mark and one control for its settings (Opus 5.5 · High), with a bolt when fast mode is on. It opens one menu that holds them all.
  • Bottom right: the context ring (Usage.used / size; amber past 80%, red past 95%), then one round button: send, or stop while a turn runs and the box is empty.
  • Waiting messages sit on a tray just above the box.
  • Gold is kept for focus and for what to press: the lit button, the caret and the slider.

What CAF decides

  • Text only (Part::Text), so there are no attachments yet.
  • A message sent during a turn waits. Send stays on, and Stop drops the waiting messages too.
  • The menu is built from Setting.form: a list of Choices or a Switch, each present only if the agent offers it.
  • A setting changed during a turn is held until the turn ends.
  • Rights: without act nothing can be sent or set; without agents there's no Learn them now.
  • Keys: Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, and nothing happens on Enter while an IME is composing.
